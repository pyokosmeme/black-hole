DE-LISTING FROM DRAFT2DIGITAL

2026.06.20 ;; #publishing #draft2digital #announcements

D2D is shifting to annual fees, and most of my distribution there was free OverDrive copies. Winding down the account; existing reader libraries should be unaffected.

i. the situation

As Draft2Digital is moving to annual fees for managing books sold through their platform, and my sales have primarily been free copies via OverDrive, I have begun the process to de-list from Draft2Digital and winding down my account there.

ii. what de-listing means

D2D describes delisting as revoking permission to distribute, and its print-status page phrases the effect as stopping new sales, and making the book no longer for sale. That is not the same thing as recalling files from customer libraries, and I hope existing readers will not be impacted.
